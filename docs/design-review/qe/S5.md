**Risk to carry into S5.c (not a defect here):** Pi GPIO now reach parts on separately switched rails. GPIO8/CE0
(ADIN_NSS) defaults to a pull-up at Pi reset (BCM283x convention, not re-verified) and SPI0 idles CS high — either can
