Layout experiment 1.a: before and after session 2

Before = the board when session 2 began (commit 293dc11, md5 f70595e5…). After = the board now (b22752f, md5 de2e14fb…). Same renderer for both (tools/render_layers.py), same regions and scale as the design review. Generated by tools/before_after.py.

The short version

Nothing moved. 138 footprints before, 138 after, none changed position, rotation or side (T1 was tried 0.3 mm east and put back). The board is 46 × 65 mm in both. What changed is copper only.

So: the things the QE and the brief measure got better, and two signal nets got longer. The SPI detour is the cost of crowding the pocket's exits (the pairs, U2/U3's fan-out vias and the Internal 2 corridors for 3V3 / 1V8 / ADIN_PWR all leave there now), and it is the strongest argument for the 2 mm of width you offered: with a reserved Internal 2 lane under U1 the SPI goes straight east to J1 as the plan said.
